An object categorizing characters by type—'Letter', 'Digit', 'Punctuation', 'Emoji', or 'Symbol'—essential for accurate text parsing and tone analysis.

## Overview

CharacterType Object categorizes individual characters based on their type, such as "Letter", "Digit", "Punctuation", "Emoji", or "Symbol". Understanding the character type is important for parsing text correctly, detecting emotive content through emojis, and handling special symbols that may influence the meaning or tone of the text.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders assign character types within the `Character` Object to facilitate proper text parsing, sentiment analysis, and detection of virtual language objects.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users benefit from the nuanced analysis of text that considers the impact of punctuation and emojis on tone and meaning.
