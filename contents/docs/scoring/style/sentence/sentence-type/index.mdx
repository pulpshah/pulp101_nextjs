An object classifying sentences by grammatical structure—'Declarative', 'Interrogative', 'Imperative', 'Exclamatory'—assisting in grammatical analysis and style understanding.

## Overview

The SentenceType Object classifies sentences based on their grammatical structure, such as "Declarative," "Interrogative," "Imperative," or "Exclamatory." This classification assists in grammatical analysis and understanding the speaker's communicative style.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders tag sentences with their types within the `Sentence` Object, aiding in syntactic analysis and influencing assessments of style and clarity.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can recognize the types of sentences used, providing insights into the speaker's communicative methods and emphasis.
