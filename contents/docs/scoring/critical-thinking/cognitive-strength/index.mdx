## Overview

Cognitive Strength Score (CSS) combines the Critical Thinking Rate (CTR) and Cognitive Dependability Score (CDS) to measure the overall robustness of the speaker's reasoning. It reflects both the likelihood of analytical thinking and the reliability of the cognitive processes, indicating the speaker's intellectual rigor.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders compute CSS as part of the Critical Thinking Scores (CTS), using it to assess the strength of the speaker's arguments and integrate it into the Persuasion Coefficient (PC).

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can view the CSS to understand the depth and reliability of the speaker's reasoning, influencing their perception of the argument's credibility.
