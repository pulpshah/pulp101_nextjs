## Overview

Cognitive Risk Score (CRS) evaluates the potential negative impact of cognitive biases and logical fallacies present in the speaker's arguments. It considers the severity of identified biases and fallacies, adjusting the overall critical thinking assessment by accounting for weaknesses in reasoning.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders calculate CRS by quantifying the severity of detected biases and fallacies in the `Argument` Object, integrating it into the Critical Thinking Scores (CTS).

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can see the CRS to recognize potential flaws in the speaker's reasoning, helping them critically evaluate the arguments presented.
