The Fallacy Object identifies logical fallacies present in the speaker's arguments, such as "Strawman" or "Ad Hominem". Each fallacy includes its name, severity, and description. Detecting fallacies is crucial for evaluating the validity of arguments and the speaker's logical reasoning, directly impacting the Critical Thinking Score and Persuasion Coefficient (PC).

## Overview

An object representing logical fallacies in arguments, including the fallacy name, severity, and description, used to assess argument validity and logical reasoning.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders implement logic analysis algorithms to detect fallacies within arguments, populating the `Fallacy` Object to quantify their impact on the Critical Thinking Score.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can view identified fallacies to understand potential weaknesses in the speaker's arguments, aiding in critical evaluation of the content.
