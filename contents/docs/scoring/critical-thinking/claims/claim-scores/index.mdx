An object containing the scores assigned to a claim—clarity, appeal, style, critical thinking, and Persuasive Coefficient—quantifying its effectiveness and impact.

## Overview

ClaimScores Object encapsulates the various scores assigned to a claim, including clarity, appeal, style, critical thinking, and the overall Persuasion Coefficient (PC) score. These scores quantify the effectiveness and impact of the claim, contributing to the calculation of the Persuasive Force.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders implement scoring algorithms that evaluate different aspects of the claim, storing the results in the `ClaimScores` Object for use in higher-level calculations like the Persuasive Force.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can view these scores to gain insights into the strengths and weaknesses of each claim, understanding how they affect the speaker's overall persuasiveness.
