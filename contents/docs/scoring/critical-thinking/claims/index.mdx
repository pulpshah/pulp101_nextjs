An object representing a statement or assertion by the speaker, including text, type, knowledge type, relevancy, risks, and scoring elements for analysis.

## Overview

The Claim Object represents a statement or assertion made by the speaker, including details such as the claim text, type (fact, value, or policy), knowledge type, relevancy, associated risks, and scoring elements like clarity and appeal. Analyzing claims is crucial for evaluating the speaker's arguments, credibility, and the overall persuasive effect.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders use the `Claim` Object to store and analyze claims, integrating it with scoring algorithms for clarity, appeal, and critical thinking, directly impacting the Persuasion Coefficient (PC).

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can explore claims to understand the speaker's main points, assess their validity, and see how they contribute to the speaker's persuasive effectiveness.
