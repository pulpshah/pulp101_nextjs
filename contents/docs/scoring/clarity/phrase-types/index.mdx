## Overview

PhraseType Object categorizes key phrases based on their grammatical structure, such as "Noun Phrase", "Verb Phrase", "Adjective Phrase", or "Adverbial Phrase". This classification aids in linguistic analysis, helping to identify significant expressions that contribute to the meaning and emphasis within the speech.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders assign phrase types within the `Key Phrase` Object, enabling targeted analysis of language use and contributing to assessments of clarity and style.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users benefit from insights into important phrases and how they enhance the speaker's message and impact.
