## Overview

Aspect refers to the grammatical feature of verbs that expresses the flow of time relative to the action, such as whether it is ongoing (progressive), completed (perfect), or habitual. In debate analysis, understanding aspect helps in parsing verb tenses and can influence the interpretation of statements, affecting clarity and style assessments.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders implement natural language processing (NLP) techniques to identify the aspect of verbs within the `Token` Object, aiding in grammatical analysis and clarity scoring.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users may not interact directly with aspect, but it affects the readability and understanding of the speaker's statements, contributing to overall clarity.
