## Overview

PartOfSpeech Object classifies tokens (words) according to their grammatical role in a sentence, such as "Noun", "Verb", "Adjective", or "Adverb". This classification is fundamental for syntactic analysis, understanding sentence structure, and contributes to assessments of clarity and linguistic style.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders tag tokens with their parts of speech using NLP techniques, enabling grammatical analysis and aiding in the evaluation of clarity and style.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users benefit indirectly through analyses that ensure proper understanding of the speaker's language and enhance the accuracy of the assessment.
