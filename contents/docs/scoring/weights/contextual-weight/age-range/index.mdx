The span of years representing the speaker's age group, providing context about their generational perspective and potential influence on different audience demographics.

## Overview

Age Range denotes the span of years representing the speaker's age group, such as "45-54" or "55-64". Understanding a speaker's age range provides context about their generational perspectives, life experiences, and potential resonance with certain demographics within the audience. It aids in tailoring the analysis to consider how age-related factors may influence the speaker's viewpoints and the audience's reception.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders include the age range in the `Demographics` Object within each `Speaker` Object. This information can be utilized to adjust Contextual Weights (CW) when considering audience demographics in the Persuasive Force calculation.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users examining debate analyses can view speakers' age ranges to better understand their perspectives and how they might connect with different age groups in the audience.
