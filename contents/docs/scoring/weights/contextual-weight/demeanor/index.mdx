## Overview

Demeanor Score assesses the speaker's outward behavior and mannerisms during their turn, reflecting attributes such as cordiality, defensiveness, aggression, and poise. It quantifies how the speaker's conduct may influence audience perception, with higher scores indicating more positive and effective demeanor traits. This score helps in understanding the impact of non-verbal cues and behavioral styles on the overall persuasiveness of the speaker.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders implement algorithms to analyze behavioral cues from the speaker's language and delivery, assigning a Demeanor Score within the `Speaker Mind-state` attributes. This involves detecting indicators of cordiality, aggression, and poise to quantify their effects on persuasion.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users reviewing debate analyses can see the Demeanor Score to understand how the speaker's behavior may have affected the audience's reception, providing insights into their interpersonal effectiveness.
