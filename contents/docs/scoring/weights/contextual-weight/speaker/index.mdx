## Overview

The Speaker Object contains comprehensive information about a participant in the conversation, including identification details, demographics, psychographics, biography, and their speaking turns. It serves as a central node for associating the speaker with their contributions and contextual attributes.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders use the `Speaker` Object to store and access all relevant data about a speaker, facilitating personalized analysis and integration into the PersuasiveForce calculation.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can learn about the speaker's background, viewpoints, and participation in the conversation, enhancing their understanding of the content and context.
