An object containing the speaker's psychological attributes—values, beliefs, and interests—providing insight into motivations and ideological perspectives.

## Overview

The Psychographics Object represents the speaker's psychological attributes, including values, beliefs, and interests. It provides insight into the speaker's motivations, ideologies, and areas of focus, which can influence their communication style and the way their messages resonate with different audience segments.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders include the `Psychographics` Object within each `Speaker` Object to enrich the contextual data used in analyzing the speaker's communication and adjusting the Contextual Weight (CW) in the Persuasive Force calculation.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can examine the speaker's psychographics to understand how their personal values and beliefs may shape their arguments and influence their persuasive strategies.
