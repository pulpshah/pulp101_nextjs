An object representing the final calculation of the speaker's persuasive impact, combining Rhetorical Weight (RW), Persuasion Coefficient (PC), and Contextual Weight (CW) to provide a comprehensive effectiveness score.

## Overview

The PersuasiveForce Object encapsulates the final calculation of the speaker's persuasive impact during a turn, combining Rhetorical Weight (RW), Persuasion Coefficient (PC), and Contextual Weight (CW). It provides a comprehensive score reflecting the overall effectiveness of the speaker's communication in persuading the audience.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders implement the calculation of Persuasive Force within this object, integrating various scores and ensuring accurate representation of the speaker's persuasive effectiveness.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can view the Persuasive Force score to quickly gauge how impactful the speaker's turn was in terms of persuasion.
