## Overview

Mood Attributes are descriptors capturing the speaker's emotional state during their turn, such as "Confident", "Anxious", "Passionate", or "Calm". These attributes help in assessing the emotional tone and can influence audience engagement and perception, contributing to the analysis of Pathos and Style Score.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders use sentiment analysis tools to identify mood attributes from the speaker's language and delivery, integrating them into the `Speaker Mind-state` and Style Score calculations.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can observe the speaker's mood attributes to understand the emotional context of their delivery and how it might affect persuasiveness.
