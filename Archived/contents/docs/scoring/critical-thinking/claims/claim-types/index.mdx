An object classifying claims as 'Fact', 'Value', or 'Policy', aiding in analyzing the claim's purpose and required support.

## Overview

ClaimType Object classifies claims into categories based on their nature: "Fact" (asserting something is true), "Value" (asserting something is good or bad), or "Policy" (advocating for a course of action). This classification aids in analyzing the claim's purpose and the type of evidence or reasoning required to support it.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders categorize claims using the `ClaimType` Object to tailor the analysis and scoring algorithms based on the claim type, as different types may require different evaluation criteria.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can see the claim type to better understand the nature of the assertion and the kind of support they should expect from the speaker.
