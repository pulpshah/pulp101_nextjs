## Overview

The Argument Object represents a coherent set of reasons or evidence presented by the speaker to support or oppose a claim. It includes details about the argument's text, associated claims, strength level, any logical fallacies or biases, and critical thinking scores. This object is crucial for analyzing the quality and effectiveness of the speaker's reasoning.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders create the `Argument Object` to encapsulate all elements of an argument, enabling detailed analysis of reasoning quality and integration with scoring algorithms like the Critical Thinking Score.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can examine arguments to understand how the speaker constructs their reasoning, assess the validity of their points, and identify any flaws or strengths in their logic.
