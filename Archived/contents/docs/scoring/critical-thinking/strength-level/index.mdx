An object categorizing the potency of an argument or claim—'Strong', 'Moderate', 'Weak'—assessing persuasive impact based on evidence, logic, and delivery.

## Overview

The StrengthLevel Object categorizes the potency of an argument or claim as "Strong," "Moderate," or "Weak." It helps in assessing the persuasive impact of the argument based on factors like evidence quality, logical coherence, and delivery effectiveness.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders assign strength levels to arguments within the `Argument` Object, influencing critical thinking assessments and the overall Persuasive Force.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can evaluate the strength of arguments to understand how compelling they are, aiding in their critical analysis of the speaker's points.
