An object categorizing potential risks of a claim or argument—'Low', 'Moderate', or 'High'—to assess likelihood of negative outcomes like inaccuracies or alienation.

## Overview

The RiskLevel Object categorizes the potential risks associated with a claim or argument, such as "Low," "Moderate," or "High." It helps in assessing the likelihood of negative outcomes like inaccuracies or alienating certain audience segments, informing the analysis of the speaker's strategic choices and their potential impact.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders assign risk levels to claims within the `Claim` Object, influencing the calculation of the Persuasion Coefficient (PC) by adjusting scores based on potential negative impacts.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can see the risk levels associated with claims to understand the potential consequences of the speaker's statements, such as the chance of factual errors or audience backlash.
