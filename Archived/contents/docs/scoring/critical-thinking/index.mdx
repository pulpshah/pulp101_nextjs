A score assessing reasoning quality, logical coherence, and analytical thinking, incorporating Critical Thinking Rate (CTR), Cognitive Dependability Score (CDS), Cognitive Strength Score (CSS), and Cognitive Risk Score (CRS) to evaluate critical thinking skills.

## Overview

Critical Thinking Score evaluates the speaker's reasoning quality, logical coherence, and ability to think analytically. It incorporates metrics like the Critical Thinking Rate (CTR), Cognitive Dependability Score (CDS), Cognitive Strength Score (CSS), and Cognitive Risk Score (CRS). A higher score indicates stronger critical thinking skills and more persuasive arguments.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders calculate the Critical Thinking Score by integrating various cognitive metrics, using it to influence the Persuasion Coefficient (PC) and overall Persuasive Force.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can examine the Critical Thinking Score to assess the logical strength and reliability of the speaker's arguments.
