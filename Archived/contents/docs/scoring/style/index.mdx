## Overview

Style Score evaluates the effectiveness of the speaker's rhetorical techniques, language use, and delivery in enhancing persuasion and engagement. It considers factors like tone, use of rhetorical devices, expressiveness, and language clarity, contributing to the overall Persuasive Force.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders develop algorithms to assess various style elements, assigning a Style Score that affects the Persuasion Coefficient (PC) and Rhetorical Weight (RW).

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can see how the speaker's style influences their persuasiveness, understanding the role of delivery and language in effective communication.
