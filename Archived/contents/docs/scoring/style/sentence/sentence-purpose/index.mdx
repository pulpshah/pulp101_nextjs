An object categorizing sentences by communicative intent—'Statement', 'Question', 'Command', 'Exclamation'—aiding in interpreting intentions and communication strategies.

## Overview

The SentencePurpose Object categorizes sentences based on their communicative intent, such as "Statement," "Question," "Command," or "Exclamation." Understanding the purpose aids in interpreting the speaker's intentions and tailoring the analysis of their communication strategies.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders assign sentence purposes within the `Sentence` Object, informing analysis related to the speaker's intention and contributing to scoring elements like clarity and style.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can understand the function of each sentence, helping them interpret the speaker's approach and effectiveness in conveying their message.
