An object representing a complete thought, including text, position, and linguistic attributes, fundamental for analyzing structure and meaning in communication.

## Overview

The Sentence Object represents a complete thought expressed by the speaker, including its text, position within the turn, and associated linguistic and semantic attributes. Analyzing sentences is fundamental for understanding the structure, content, and meaning of the speaker's communication, contributing to various scoring elements.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders parse the speaker's turns into sentences, attaching linguistic and semantic data to each `Sentence` Object for detailed analysis, affecting clarity, style, and critical thinking assessments.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can read and analyze individual sentences to understand specific points made by the speaker and how they contribute to the overall message.
