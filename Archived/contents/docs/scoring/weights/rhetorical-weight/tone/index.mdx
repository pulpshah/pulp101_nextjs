## Overview

The Tone Object identifies the overall attitude or emotional quality conveyed by the speaker, such as "Neutral," "Sarcastic," "Aggressive," or "Friendly." Analyzing tone helps in understanding the speaker's approach and how it may affect the audience's reception and engagement.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders determine the tone of the speech using NLP techniques, integrating it into analyses of mood, style, and Rhetorical Weight (RW).

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can perceive the speaker's tone to better interpret the message and its potential impact on the audience.
