## Overview

The Sentiment Object measures the emotional tone of the speaker's communication, quantifying polarity (negative to positive) and subjectivity (objective to subjective). It provides insight into the overall emotional disposition conveyed, affecting audience perception and engagement.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders implement sentiment analysis algorithms to populate the `Sentiment` Object for sentences or larger text segments, contributing to the evaluation of mood, tone, and emotional impact.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can understand the emotional undertones of the speaker's message, helping them interpret the intent and potential influence on the audience.
