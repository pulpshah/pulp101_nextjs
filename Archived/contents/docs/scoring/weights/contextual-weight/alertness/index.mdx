A measure of the speaker's energy and attentiveness, ranging from negative to positive values, indicating how their physical and mental state affects delivery and audience perception.

## Overview

Alertness Score measures the speaker's level of energy and attentiveness during their turn. It ranges from negative values indicating low alertness (e.g., "tired", "exhausted") to positive values indicating high alertness (e.g., "energized", "alert"). This score helps assess how the speaker's physical and mental state may affect their delivery and the audience's perception.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

oders implement algorithms to evaluate vocal cues, speech patterns, and content for signs of alertness or fatigue, assigning an alertness score within the `Speaker Mind-state` attributes.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can observe the speaker's alertness score to understand how energetic or engaged the speaker was during their turn, influencing their interpretation of the speaker's effectiveness.
