## Overview

The Intention Object identifies the speaker's underlying purpose or goal in delivering a particular sentence or message, such as "Inform", "Persuade", "Criticize", or "Motivate". Understanding the intention aids in interpreting the speaker's strategies and evaluating the effectiveness of their communication in achieving desired outcomes.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders use natural language understanding techniques to determine the speaker's intention, incorporating it into the analysis of sentences and contributing to the calculation of Persuasive Force.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can comprehend the speaker's motives behind statements, enhancing their understanding of the message and its potential impact.
