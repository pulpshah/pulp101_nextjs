An object categorizing the nature of a turn—'Opening Statement', 'Response', 'Rebuttal'—aiding in analyzing objectives and evaluating contributions.

## Overview

The TurnType Object categorizes the nature of a turn in the conversation, such as "Opening Statement," "Response," "Rebuttal," "Question," or "Closing Statement." This classification helps in analyzing the speaker's objectives and tailoring the evaluation of their contributions.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders assign turn types within the `Turn` Object, informing the analysis of the speaker's strategies and adjusting scoring elements accordingly.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can understand the purpose of each turn, providing context for the speaker's remarks and the flow of the conversation.
