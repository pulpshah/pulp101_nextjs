## Overview

Mind-state encompasses the speaker's psychological and emotional condition during their turn, including mood attributes, demeanor, and alertness. It provides insights into the speaker's internal state, which can influence their delivery, choice of words, and overall persuasiveness.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders gather data on mood attributes, demeanor scores, and alertness to construct the speaker's mind-state, affecting analyses of style and effectiveness.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can understand how the speaker's mental and emotional state may have impacted their performance and the audience's perception.
