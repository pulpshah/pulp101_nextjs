## Overview

The Topic Object represents the main subjects discussed by the speaker, including the topic name, subtopics, keywords, related references, and an importance score. Understanding topics is crucial for contextual analysis, relevancy assessments, and adjusting the Contextual Weight (CW) in the Persuasive Force calculation.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders identify and structure topics within the `Topic` Object, linking them to sentences and claims to assess relevancy and adjust Contextual Weight (CW).

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can explore the topics addressed to understand the focus of the speaker's message and its relevance to their interests.
