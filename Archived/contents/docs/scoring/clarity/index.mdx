An assessment of the effectiveness of communication based on explicitness, concision, focus, cohesion, and coherence, indicating how clear and understandable the message is.

## Overview

Clarity Score measures how effectively the speaker communicates their message, focusing on explicitness, concision, focus, cohesion, and coherence. A higher clarity score indicates that the speaker's ideas are presented in a clear, understandable, and logically structured manner, enhancing the audience's comprehension.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders develop scoring algorithms that analyze linguistic features and structural elements to assign a clarity score, which is included in the `ClaimScores` Object and affects the Persuasion Coefficient (PC).

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can view the clarity score to understand how well the speaker conveyed their ideas, influencing their perception of the speaker's communication skills.
