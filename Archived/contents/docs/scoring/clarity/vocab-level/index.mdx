An object categorizing words or phrases by complexity—'Easy', 'Intermediate', 'Advanced'—assessing language accessibility and audience suitability.

## Overview

The VocabularyLevel Object categorizes words or phrases based on their complexity and familiarity, such as "Easy," "Intermediate," "Advanced," or "Expert." This classification helps in assessing the accessibility of the language used and its suitability for the intended audience.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders classify vocabulary levels within the `Feeling` Object or `Token` Object, contributing to evaluations of clarity and style.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can understand the language complexity, determining how accessible the speaker's message is to various audience segments.
