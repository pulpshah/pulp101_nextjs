An object capturing nuanced emotions expressed by the speaker, including the feeling name and vocabulary level, aiding in emotional analysis and resonance assessment.

## Overview

The Feeling Object captures nuanced emotions or feelings expressed by the speaker, such as "Hopeful", "Empathetic", or "Concerned", along with the vocabulary level used to convey them. This object helps in analyzing the depth and subtlety of emotional expression, contributing to the assessment of Pathos and the emotional resonance of the message.

## Applications

### 1) Coder's Perspective

Coders extract nuanced feelings using advanced sentiment analysis, associating them with the `Emotion` Object to enhance the understanding of the speaker's emotional appeal.

### 2) User's Perspective

Users can see the specific feelings conveyed, gaining insights into the emotional nuances of the speaker's delivery and how it might affect audience engagemen
